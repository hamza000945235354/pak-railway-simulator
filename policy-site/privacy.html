Privacy Policy

Pak Railway Simulator · Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Pak Railway Simulator (the “Game”) uses, why, and the choices you have. The Game is an unofficial train-driving game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Pakistan Railways or any government body.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress, coins, unlocked routes and locomotives, settings (language, sound, camera, game speed) and tutorial status are saved only on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes this information.

2. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. To confirm a purchase and restore it on a new device, purchase records (product ID, purchase token, time of purchase) and a random anonymous app user ID are processed by our purchase-verification provider, RevenueCat (privacy policy).

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos (for example, to continue a journey or double a reward) and banner ads. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Buying “Remove ads” turns off banner ads.

4. Crash reports and diagnostics

If enabled in the Game, Google Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics collect crash logs, device model, Android version, app version and anonymous usage events (for example, which mission was played) so we can fix bugs and improve the Game. This data is not used to identify you personally. See Firebase privacy information.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • There is no chat or user-generated content in the Game.

6. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide and restore purchases and to meet legal and tax obligations. To ask us to delete purchase or diagnostic data linked to your device, email us at hamzasiddique951@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.

8. Security

Data sent to Google, RevenueCat and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
